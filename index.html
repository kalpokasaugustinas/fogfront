How to play

  • The map is split into segments. Each one you own grows troops up to its cap. Bigger segments grow faster and hold more.
  • Tap your segment to select it, then tap a neighbour to send the chosen % of troops: an attack on enemy/neutral, a move to your own. Tap the selected segment again to deselect.
  • Drag from your segment to any neighbour, including your own, to send troops there.
  • Fight: attack = troops × attack bonus, defense = defenders × defense bonus. Higher number wins; the winner keeps the difference.
  • Neutral (grey) segments don't grow. Bots fight each other too: let them weaken each other.
  • Each segment can hold one building, paid with its own troops. Captured buildings switch sides.

    Win: wipe out every enemy faction. Keys: space = pause, 1–4 = send %, Esc = deselect.
