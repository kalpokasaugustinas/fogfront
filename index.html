How to play

  • The map is split into segments. Each one you own grows 1% of its cap per second up to the cap (largest tile: 400). Bigger tiles grow faster and hold more.
  • Tap your segment to select it, then tap a neighbour to send the chosen % of troops: an attack on enemy/neutral, a move to your own. Tap the selected segment again to deselect.
  • Drag from your segment to any neighbour, including your own, to send troops there.
  • 🔁 Auto on: sending to your own neighbour also sets a route. Every second the source tile forwards all troops above its reserve (100% − send %, of its cap) to the target. Chain routes to pipe troops from the back to the front. Select a tile and tap ✕ Stop to end its route.
  • Fight: attackers stop at the border and fight for 1–3 seconds. Attack = troops × attack bonus, defense = defenders × defense bonus. Higher number wins; the winner keeps the difference.
  • Lakes and mountains can't be occupied or crossed.
  • Terrain: 🏔️ Highland (next to mountains) defenders +30% · 🌲 Forest defenders +15% · 🌊 River +25% growth · 💧 Lakeside +15% growth.
  • ✨ Relic tiles: a few neutral tiles hold a shrine with a perk (shown above the number) and a stronger garrison. Whoever holds the tile gets the perk; lose the tile, lose the perk.
  • Neutral (grey) tiles start with a garrison that matches their size and regrow back to it (1% of cap per second) if you only damage them. Bots fight each other too: let them weaken each other.
  • Each segment can hold one building, paid with its own troops. Captured buildings switch sides.

    🧭 Expedition (roguelite): 5 stages from a valley to the whole world. Win a stage and everything you hold collapses into one capital on a bigger map: all your people come along (the capital fills up, the rest waits in a 🏕️ reserve), and each building type becomes a capital bonus = its effect × the share of your segments that had it (4 farms of 10 segments → +60% × 40% = +24% growth). Nothing can be built in a capital. Bots start with part of your strength and their own capitals. Pick a perk at every zoom-out. Lose once and the run ends.

    Win: wipe out every enemy faction. Keys: space = pause, 1–4 = send %, A = auto toggle, Esc = deselect.
